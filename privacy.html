Privacy Policy

How we collect, store and use your personal data.

121 Driving Tuition respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, store and use your personal data in accordance with UK GDPR.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is 121 Driving Tuition, Milton Keynes, UK.
Contact: licence4you@yahoo.co.uk

2. What Data We Collect

  • Full name
  • Email address and phone number (for contact and booking confirmations)
  • Lesson start address (usually the student’s residence) — necessary for providing the service
  • Booking details (lesson dates, instructor, vehicle)

Note: We do not process or store payment information. All payments are made directly to the selected instructor’s account.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

  • To organise and manage driving lessons, including planning routes and collecting students from the provided address
  • To communicate regarding changes or confirmations of bookings

4. Legal Basis

Data processing is in accordance with GDPR and UK GDPR, based on the user’s consent and necessity for the performance of the service (lesson delivery).

5. Data Sharing

Data may only be shared with instructors providing lessons or with legal authorities if required by law. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at licence4you@yahoo.co.uk.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations. Once no longer needed, your data will be securely deleted.
