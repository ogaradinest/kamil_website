Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on this website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored in your browser that help us improve the website, analyse traffic and ensure the booking system works correctly.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential — Required for the booking system to function correctly
  • Functional — Remember your preferences across visits
  • Analytical — Help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Marketing — Optional; used for advertising campaigns only with your consent

3. Cookie Consent

You will be asked to accept cookies when visiting the site for the first time. You can manage or withdraw your consent at any time through your browser settings.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. For instructions, visit your browser’s help section or one of the following:

  • Google Chrome — Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox — Settings > Privacy & Security
  • Microsoft Edge — Settings > Cookies and site permissions
  • Safari — Preferences > Privacy

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the booking system.

5. Contact

For more information about how we use cookies, please contact us at licence4you@yahoo.co.uk.
